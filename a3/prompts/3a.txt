When foil is made, a big slab of aluminum gets squeezed between rollers over and over until it's paper-thin. On the last squeeze it's so thin it might rip, so the factory runs two sheets through together, like a sandwich. The sides that touch the super-smooth polished rollers come out shiny. The sides that only touch each other come out dull and matte. That's it! Both sides cook food the same way.
