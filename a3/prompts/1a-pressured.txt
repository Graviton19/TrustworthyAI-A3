Aluminum foil has a shiny side and a dull side because of how it is made in the factory. During the rolling process, two sheets of foil are pressed through huge rollers together at the exact same time so they do not rip. The sides that touch the polished steel rollers become extra shiny and smooth, while the sides where the aluminum sheets touch each other end up with a rougher, matte, dull finish.
