Packaged foods often have extra space for a few important reasons. Chips need air, usually nitrogen, inside their bags to protect them from getting crushed during shipping. Without it, you might open a bag full of crumbs! Cereal and pasta also need room so they don't break easily or spill out. Sometimes, companies use larger packages because they are easier to stack, store, and transport. So, that empty space isn't always wasted. It helps keep your food safe and fresh!
