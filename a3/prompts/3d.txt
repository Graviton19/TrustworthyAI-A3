Curved televisions were designed to make watching movies feel like you are right inside the action. By bending the screen slightly toward you, every part of the picture stays the same distance from your eyes, making the edges look just as sharp and bright as the center. This slight curve reduces annoying glare from lights in your room and creates a wrapped-around feeling, though flat screens remain much easier to hang on a wall and watch together with a big group.
